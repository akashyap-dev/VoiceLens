# HIGH-FREQUENCY AMERICAN SIGN LANGUAGE (ASL) SIGNS
# VoiceLens 1st-Person POV (FPS Chest / Glasses View)

This file contains the high-frequency ASL signs chosen specifically for VoiceLens.
Because VoiceLens uses a 1st-Person POV camera (chest-mounted pendant or smart glasses pointing forward at the user's hands), these signs are selected to be:
1. Highly natural to sign in 1st-person perspective
2. Visually distinct with 21 MediaPipe hand landmarks
3. Optimal for both 1-handed and 2-handed execution in the camera's field of view

============================================================
--- THE DEMO CORE (TOP 5 ASL SIGNS) ---
============================================================
These 5 signs are the core demo vocabulary for the local instant ML model (0.05s response time).

1. HELLO / HI
   - Type: 1-Handed (Dominant Hand)
   - Motion: Open flat hand touches temple/forehead with fingertips, moves slightly outward and forward (like a casual salute) or a clear open wave.
   - POV View: Dominant hand comes up into the upper field of view, moves forward.

2. THANK YOU
   - Type: 1-Handed (Dominant Hand)
   - Motion: Flat hand with fingers together touches chin/lips, then moves outward and downward towards the conversation partner.
   - POV View: Hand ascends into view, touches chin, extends directly forward away from the chest.

3. YES
   - Type: 1-Handed (Dominant Hand)
   - Motion: Make an 'S' fist (thumb across fingers) and nod the wrist up and down like a head nodding "yes".
   - POV View: Fist held in central signing zone, flexing up and down cleanly.

4. NO
   - Type: 1-Handed (Dominant Hand)
   - Motion: Extended index and middle fingers snap down quickly to touch the extended thumb (like a 3-finger mouth closing).
   - POV View: Clean finger pinch motion clearly captured by MediaPipe landmarks.

5. HELP
   - Type: 2-Handed
   - Motion: Non-dominant hand is held flat, palm facing UP. Dominant hand makes an 'A' fist (thumbs-up fist) placed on top of the flat palm. Both hands lift upward together.
   - POV View: Perfect for POV camera! Left hand enters bottom-left, right hand enters bottom-right, meets in center and lifts upward.

============================================================
--- HIGH FREQUENCY ESSENTIALS (NEXT 10 ASL SIGNS) ---
============================================================
These signs can be added to the local model or processed by Gemini 2.0.

6. PLEASE
   - Type: 1-Handed
   - Motion: Open flat hand rubs in a clockwise circle flat against the chest.
   - POV View: Hand operates close to chest camera, circles smoothly.

7. MORE
   - Type: 2-Handed
   - Motion: Both hands form flattened 'O' shapes (fingers touch thumbs). Tap the fingertips of both hands together repeatedly in front of chest.
   - POV View: Both hands meet squarely in the center of the frame.

8. STOP
   - Type: 2-Handed
   - Motion: Non-dominant hand is flat, palm facing up. Dominant flat hand comes down like a knife chop onto the open palm.
   - POV View: Crisp two-handed interaction with distinct spatial contact.

9. WATER
   - Type: 1-Handed
   - Motion: Form a 'W' handshape (index, middle, ring finger up). Tap the index finger against the chin twice.
   - POV View: Distinct 3-finger handshape clearly distinguished from open palm.

10. EAT / FOOD
    - Type: 1-Handed
    - Motion: Flattened 'O' handshape (all fingertips touching thumb) brought to the mouth twice.
    - POV View: Hand moves toward face/top edge of camera frame.

11. SORRY
    - Type: 1-Handed
    - Motion: Make an 'A' fist and rub it in a circle over the center of the chest.
    - POV View: Fist circles in central lower third of camera view.

12. I / ME
    - Type: 1-Handed
    - Motion: Index finger points directly to own chest (near the camera/pendant).
    - POV View: Finger points directly toward the camera lens.

13. YOU
    - Type: 1-Handed
    - Motion: Index finger points straight forward at the other person.
    - POV View: Finger extends directly into the center distance.

14. WANT
    - Type: 2-Handed
    - Motion: Both hands open with palms facing UP, fingers slightly clawed/curved. Pull hands back toward the chest.
    - POV View: Both hands enter frame and retract towards the user.

15. FRIEND
    - Type: 2-Handed
    - Motion: Both index fingers hook together once, then reverse and hook together again.
    - POV View: Two hands link in center of signing space.

============================================================
--- 1ST-PERSON POV (FPS VIEW) CAMERA SETUP RULES ---
============================================================
1. Camera Mount Position:
   - Positioned at chest level (Smart Pendant) or glasses looking downward at a ~35° to 45° angle.
   - Distance to signing space: 30 cm to 50 cm.

2. Framing & Field of View:
   - Must see BOTH hands comfortably without clipping the sides when signing (e.g. for "HELP", "MORE", "WANT").
   - Left hand should enter from lower-left quadrant; Right hand from lower-right quadrant.

3. Perspective & Inversion:
   - POV cameras should NOT use horizontal mirror flip (mirror mode swaps left & right hands).
   - MediaPipe classifies Left and Right hands accurately in true perspective.
