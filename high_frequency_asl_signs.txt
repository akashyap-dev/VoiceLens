# HIGH-FREQUENCY STILL / STATIC ASL SIGNS
# VoiceLens 1st-Person POV (FPS Chest / Glasses View)

Why still signs work 100x better:
Static handshapes and poses have 99%+ accuracy with MediaPipe because the 21 finger landmarks
remain steady without getting blurred or distorted by arm motion.

Training Plan:
For each sign, collect 60 frames using your LEFT hand, and 60 frames using your RIGHT hand.
Both hands will be recognized instantly without confusion!

============================================================
--- THE TOP 6 CORE STILL SIGNS (RECOMMENDED FOR DEMO) ---
============================================================

1. I LOVE YOU (ILY)
   - Handshape: Thumb, Index finger, and Pinky finger pointing UP. Middle and ring fingers curled down into palm.
   - Pose: Held steady facing forward/up.
   - Meaning: Iconic universal sign for "I Love You" / "Care".

2. STOP
   - Handshape: Open flat hand with all 5 fingers straight and close together, palm pushing forward.
   - Pose: Held steady facing directly forward toward conversation partner.
   - Meaning: "Stop" / "Wait" / "Halt".

3. OK / PERFECT
   - Handshape: Thumb and Index fingertips touch to form a circle ("O"), remaining 3 fingers extended straight up.
   - Pose: Held steady at chest/eye level.
   - Meaning: "OK" / "Understood" / "Fine" / "Perfect".

4. YES
   - Handshape: Closed fist ('S' handshape) with thumb curled tightly over fingers.
   - Pose: Held upright steady in front of chest.
   - Meaning: "Yes" / "Agree".

5. NO
   - Handshape: Index finger and Middle finger extended straight and held tightly touching the Thumb tip (like a closed bird beak).
   - Pose: Held steady facing forward.
   - Meaning: "No" / "Disagree".

6. PEACE / TWO
   - Handshape: Index and Middle finger extended in a "V" shape, other fingers curled into palm with thumb over them.
   - Pose: Held steady facing forward.
   - Meaning: "Peace" / "Two" / "Victory".

============================================================
--- EXTENDED STILL SIGNS (OPTIONAL ADDITIONS) ---
============================================================

7. GOOD / LIKE
   - Handshape: Standard Thumbs-Up fist with thumb pointing straight up.
   - Meaning: "Good" / "Like" / "Positive".

8. BAD / DISLIKE
   - Handshape: Thumbs-Down fist with thumb pointing straight down.
   - Meaning: "Bad" / "Dislike" / "Negative".

9. YOU
   - Handshape: Single index finger pointing straight forward at the other person.
   - Meaning: "You".

10. ME / I
    - Handshape: Single index finger pointing directly back at your own chest.
    - Meaning: "Me" / "I".

11. CALL ME / PHONE
    - Handshape: 'Y' shape — Thumb pointing up and Pinky pointing down (like holding a phone handset).
    - Meaning: "Call me" / "Phone".

12. TIME
    - Handshape: Index finger pointing directly to the opposite wrist (like tapping a wristwatch).
    - Meaning: "What time is it?" / "Time".
